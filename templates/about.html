About Popy Crunch

Popy Crunch is a local snack brand offering crispy and thin mini popia in two signature flavours: Cheese and Salted Egg. We aim to provide an affordable and convenient snack that brings a satisfying crunch to every bite.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide affordable, delicious and convenient snacks for everyone.

👁️

Our Vision

To grow Popy Crunch into a recognizable local snack brand.

Our Product

Crispy mini popia available in Cheese and Salted Egg flavours.
